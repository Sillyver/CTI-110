# PB&J Sandwich Robot

## Assumptions

- Fully charged humanoid robot
- A table setup with
    - Two slices of bread side by side on a paper plate
    - Peanut butter
    - Jelly
    - Butter knife
- In a kitchen
-----
## Step by Step

1. Posistion self in front of items
2. Open the Peanut butter jar by twisting lid 
3. Open the Jelly jar by twisting the lid
4. Grab knife
5. Insert knife into peanut butter jar till half way of the blade
6. Scoop Peanut butter using the knife
7. Using other hand pick up a slice of a bread
8. Use the knife to spread the peanut butter on one side of the bread
9. place unspread side of bread onto the plate
10. Insert knife into Jelly jar till half way to the blade
11. Scoop Jelly using the knife
12. Using other hand pick up the other slice of bread
13. Use the knife to spread the jelly on one side of the bread
14. Place knife on the edge of the plate
15. Place spread side of jelly bread onto the spread side of the peanut butter bread.
16. Grab knife 
17. Cut the sandwich evenly vertically
18. Place knife in sink
19. Retwist lids onto the original jars
20. Return jar to original posistion
-----

## Completion Conditons

1. Sandwich is completed
2. Sandwich is cut evenly vertically
3. Knive is in sink
4. Jar have lids and are in their original postions
